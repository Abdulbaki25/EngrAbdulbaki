Introduction :
The Lecture Timetable and Reminder App is a web-based application designed to help students manage their academic schedules effectively. The application provides a platform where students can view their lecture timetables, check lecture times, course details, venues and lecturers' information.
The system is developed to reduce the problem of missing lectures, forgetting lecture schedules and having difficulty keeping track of academic activities. It allows students to receive reminders and notifications before their lectures begin, helping them prepare for classes on time.
The application also makes it easier to organize daily academic activities, manage lecture schedules and improve time management. By using this system, students can stay informed about their lectures and develop better academic planning habits.

Aim of the Project :

The aim of this project is to design and develop a Lecture Timetable and Reminder App that helps students organize their lecture schedules, receive timely reminders and manage their academic activities efficiently.

Objective of the project:

To develop a user-friendly application for managing lecture timetables.
To enable students to view their daily and weekly lecture schedules.
To provide automatic reminders and notifications before lectures begin.
To display important lecture information such as course codes, course titles, venues and lecturers' names.
To reduce the chances of students missing lectures due to forgetfulness.
